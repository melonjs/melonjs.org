# Part 3: Building the game

Right now your project shows "Hello World!" on a grey background. Three things
turn that into a platformer, and each one lives in a different kind of file, so
each one uses a different tool.

- [Part 3-1: The art](/tutorial/part-3-1-modifying-the-art) is images: your
  tileset and your character's animation frames.
- [Part 3-2: The level](/tutorial/part-3-2-modifying-the-level) is a map you
  draw in Tiled and load in one line.
- [Part 3-3: The code](/tutorial/part-3-3-modifying-the-code) is what makes the
  character move when you press a key.

Do them in order. Part 3-2 is the one that makes your game look like a game, and
Part 3-1 is optional, so skip ahead if you want to see something on screen
sooner.
